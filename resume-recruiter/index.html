Minimum expectations for success

We are proving this with real recruits before charging. Incomplete intake = no search run.

  • Legal name, email, phone, and mailing address
  • Current résumé (PDF preferred) — truthful dates and titles only
  • Professional summary (4–8 sentences): who you are and what you want next
  • 3–5 target job titles and location rules (LinkedIn URL optional)
  • Honest tool levels for anything you claim (we will NO-GO rather than fake)
  • Named deal-breakers (industry, commute, travel, sponsorship)
  • You apply yourself within 48 hours of a GO packet and reply Applied or Passed

Minimum expectations for interns

Incomplete intake = no search run. Projects and coursework count as proof when work history is thin.

  • Legal name, email, phone, and mailing address
  • School / program and expected graduation date
  • Résumé (PDF preferred) — or a real project / portfolio link if the résumé is light
  • Short note (3–5 sentences): why this internship and what you can do now
  • Availability window (term + hours) and pay rules (stipend / unpaid OK?)
  • Target functions + location rules; honest skill levels (coursework vs used vs daily)
  • You apply yourself within 48 hours of a GO packet and reply Applied or Passed
