The thesis

From snapshot to system.

A diagnostic produces a still photograph of your business at a point in time. Pulse turns it into a moving picture — refreshed daily, scored automatically, and presented to each role in the form they need to act on.

Most small businesses run on a stack of generic tools — a CRM that wasn't built for them, a spreadsheet that ages out, an HRIS that holds data nobody can query. The result: insights live in three places, decisions get delayed, and money gets left on the table because nobody saw it in time.

The database is the layer that pulls it all together — built on top of the systems you already run, surfaced through dashboards designed for who's actually using them. It scores every client on profitability and compliance. Tracks every relationship from prospect to renewal. Drives the alerts your team needs and the reports leadership reviews. Talks to your existing systems via API; could replace pieces if and when that makes sense.

This isn't a Phase 2 conversation or a follow-on engagement. The database is part of the diagnostic engagement itself — built into the work, not bolted onto the end.
