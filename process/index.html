Each stage, in detail

What actually happens, stage by stage.

01

Discover

Stage 01 · Operational learning

We meet the people. We walk the operation. We read the systems and the contracts. We don't show up with a template — we show up with questions, and the questions are good ones because we've done this before in adjacent industries. By the close of this stage, we know your business well enough to be useful.

What you get
  • Stakeholder interviews completed across leadership and key roles
  • Inventory of systems, data sources, and current tooling
  • Initial map of the four pain areas (compliance, pricing, portfolio, operations)
  • Confidentiality and engagement protections in place
02

Diagnose

Stage 02 · Score and rank

We score every client in your book. We score every category of compliance exposure. We dollarize the renegotiation candidates. We surface the onboarding failure modes. The diagnostic produces actual numbers — not adjectives. By the close of this stage, you know exactly where the leverage is.

What you get
  • Compliance Risk Inventory across 20 categories, scored Green/Yellow/Red
  • Client Portfolio Audit with every active client scored on profitability and risk
  • Five-plus renegotiation candidates identified and dollar-quantified
  • Top 3 onboarding failure modes identified with root-cause analysis
03

Design

Stage 03 · Build the frameworks + the database schema

Now we design the fixes — and the database that holds them. Pricing tier structure. Renegotiation talking points. Onboarding playbook with owner matrix. Compliance remediation plan. Database schema architected against your existing stack and the data your team actually needs to act on. Each is a working document or working system your team can use — not a deck nobody opens twice.

What you get
  • Pricing & Service-Tier Framework with à la carte schedule for premium add-ons
  • Renegotiation Roadmap with talking points per client cohort
  • Onboarding Playbook with SOPs, checklists, and 30/60/90-day verification cadence
  • Tier 1 / Tier 2 compliance remediation plan
  • Database schema designed against your existing stack, ready to deploy in Stage 04
04

Deliver

Stage 04 · Deploy the database, implement, and train

We don't hand you a binder. We deploy the database, populated with your client roster and tuned to your team's daily workflow. We implement the top two failure modes from the onboarding diagnostic. We train operations staff on the new playbook and the new system. We support the first two renegotiation conversations directly, with the database surfacing the candidates and the math live in the room. The engagement closes with the system running, not pending.

What you get
  • Database deployed to production, populated with your roster, tuned to your stack
  • Top 2 of 3 onboarding failure modes implemented and live
  • Operations staff trained and signed off on new workflow and new system
  • Direct support for the first 1–2 renegotiation conversations
  • Final handoff binder with all artifacts, training materials, and next-step recommendations
05

Compound

Stage 05 · Optional ongoing partnership

The diagnostic ends. The work continues. A 30-day adoption check-in is included in every engagement. From there, two paths: ongoing fractional partnership (quarterly intelligence and execution support), or the Pulse platform build (turning the diagnostic into a live operating system). Both are decided after the engagement has delivered, not before.

What's possible from here
  • 30-day adoption check-in (included in original fee)
  • Tactical remediation sprints for additional fixes ($10K–$25K each)
  • Pulse intelligence platform build for ongoing visibility
  • Fractional retainer for embedded ongoing partnership
