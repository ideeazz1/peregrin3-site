How JobSwoop works

Your job search,
with a wingmate.

You bring your story. We scout for opportunities and help you get ready to apply. Here’s the flight plan.

Your storyThe scoutYour packetYour move
  1. 01

    Ready for takeoff

    Tell us about you.

    Fill out the JobSwoop form. Add your résumé, the work you want, where you can work, and what matters to you.

    First job? Projects, classes, and internships count. This form starts your search; it isn’t an application to an employer.

  2. 02

    Eyes on the opportunity

    We scout. You keep living.

    Think of a peregrine scanning the horizon. JobSwoop looks for live openings, then checks the role against your experience, skills, and must-haves.

    A promising fit gets a closer look. Missing skills or experience stay visible, so you know where you stand.

  3. 03

    Special delivery

    Your job packet lands.

    When a role fits, an email brings the opportunity and your application materials together. Here’s what’s inside.

  4. 04

    Make your landing

    Review it. Make it yours. Apply.

    Check the details, make any edits, and follow the job’s application instructions. Aim to apply within 48 hours, then reply Applied or Passed so we know what happened.

    You choose and submit every application. Employers make the hiring decisions. Our goal is to help you show up prepared for your next opportunity.

Before you take off

When will I hear about a job?

Once your profile is complete, we review it and look for suitable openings. Packets arrive when a live role fits; there isn’t a guaranteed daily email. Internship searches follow internship hiring cycles.

Does JobSwoop apply for me?

You stay in control. We prepare the packet. You review it, submit the application, and decide whether to send any outreach messages.

What does it cost?

There’s no fee during our pilot.
